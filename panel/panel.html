1 · Look up entity names

Data management shows the AOT name (e.g. RMTaxRegistrationOnCustomerEntity). The Power Automate Fin & Ops connector lists the public collection name, which the developer sets freely and which can look nothing like it (RMCustomerRegisterations). Paste any of the three names, or a misspelling, one per line, and get all three back.
